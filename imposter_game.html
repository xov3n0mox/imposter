IMPOSTER GAME

Made by pk112
Rules: Imposter gets an alternate word without knowing!
Made by pk112

How to Play

1. Secret Word Distribution: Pass the device to each player. Everyone gets a secret word. Most players get the Majority Word (e.g., "Apple"), while the Imposter(s) get an alternate word from the same category (e.g., "Pear").

💡 Crucial Twist: The Imposter DOES NOT know they are the imposter! They think their word is the standard word.

2. Directed Hints Phase: The app will randomly choose which player gives a hint next. Give a subtle, one-word or short hint about your assigned word.

3. Discussion & Reveal: Once everyone has given a hint, debate who had a slightly strange word hint! Vote and reveal the true Imposter(s).
